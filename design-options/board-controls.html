Board controls

Two ways to make the controls on the home boards clearer. The rule in both: where a control sits tells you what it changes. Anything that changes all three boards sits in one place, above them. Anything that changes one board sits on that board, in its colour. Try the switches: pick Your Choice to see the shuffle buttons appear.

A. One console

Search, filters and the board order live together in one panel, each row named for what it does.

  • Order the boards appears once, not three times. Today the same switch sits on every board, but it changes all of them.
  • Only men I follow moves in with the other filters, since it filters the boards like search and age do.
  • Who's trending becomes a link: it opens another page, so it shouldn't look like a switch.
  • The board names under the console say Jump to, so they read as shortcuts, not filters.
  • Top 12 becomes Show 12 more under the cards it adds to.

B. A bar that follows you

The toolbar keeps search and filters. Under it, a slim bar stays at the top as you scroll: which board you're on, and the order.

  • The boards are long, so the order switch and board names stay in reach the whole way down.
  • The board names become tabs: the one you're scrolling through lights up, so it's clear they're places, not filters.
  • Have your say shrinks to one line of help under the bar, with the trending link at its end.
  • Each board keeps only its own controls: its count, and in Your Choice its deal button.
