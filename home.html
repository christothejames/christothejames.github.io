How we score

Each man gets 0–100 in six categories. His overall score is the average. Scores are our editorial opinion, based on the public record linked on every profile.

Where there's no public record either way, a category gets a neutral 75. Charges are not convictions: we say when someone denies them. "Everyday men" are composites, not real individuals.
