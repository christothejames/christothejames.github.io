PRIVACY

What we keep about you, why, and for how long. Last updated 15 November 2026.

THE SHORT VERSION

  • No ads. No tracking. No analytics. We set no cookies.
  • We never ask for or keep your name or email.
  • If you request a man to be ranked, we keep a scrambled code, not who you are, so each person counts once.
  • Things you save on this site (reactions, skills, your home plan, test results) stay in your own browser. We never see them.
  • Only if you choose Save with Google on the Skills page, we keep your skills list under a scrambled code so it follows you to other devices. You can delete it any time.
  • Votes (↑ and ↓ on the boards) need a Google sign-in, so each account votes once. We keep each vote under a scrambled code, never your name or email, and show only the totals.
  • If you make a profile, we keep the username you pick and the men you follow. Only you can see it, and you can delete it any time.

WHO RUNS THIS SITE

humbored.com is an independent site run by one person. Contact: humbored.contact@gmail.com. Write to this address for any privacy question or request.

JUST VISITING

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub's servers see your IP address and what your browser asks for, and may keep this in short-term logs for security. We don't get those logs. GitHub's privacy statement.

Our fonts are stored on humbored.com itself, so just reading the site doesn't contact Google.

A few men have no free photo, so we show their own public profile picture instead, loaded straight from Facebook (Meta) or, for TikTok pictures, through unavatar.io. When one of those pictures appears, that service sees your IP address. We don't keep anything.

Why: to deliver and show the website (legitimate interest, GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)).

THE MEN ON THE BOARDS

Every profile is built from information that is already public: mostly Wikipedia and Wikidata, plus news reports and official records such as court rulings and honours lists. Each fact links to where it came from, and photos come from Wikimedia Commons with their author and licence. We don't collect anything private about these men: no contact details, health or family information beyond what those public sources say. Many profiles are added automatically from these sources and haven't been checked one by one by a person; their board comes from the documented facts, and the arrows on each man let you add your own view. If something about a man is wrong or out of date, write to humbored.contact@gmail.com and we'll correct or remove it.

Why: to report on public figures from public sources (legitimate interest, GDPR Art. 6(1)(f), and freedom of expression, Art. 85).

SEARCHING FOR A MAN

When you type 3 or more letters into the search, your browser also looks the words up on Wikipedia and Wikidata to find men who aren't on the boards yet. Wikimedia sees what you typed and your IP address. We don't keep your searches. Wikimedia's privacy policy.

SIGNING IN WITH GOOGLE

Signing in is optional. Google's sign-in script only loads once the request box appears, when you press an arrow to vote, Log in or Sign up, or when you press SAVE WITH GOOGLE on the Skills page (and on later visits if you stayed signed in), not on every visit. When you sign in, Google tells us a permanent ID for your account, and we turn it straight into a scrambled code. Google will know you signed in to humbored.com. So you stay logged in as you move around the site, we then give your browser a random login code. It's kept in your browser and on our server (next to your scrambled code, nothing else) for 30 days, or until you press Log out, which deletes it in both places. The Skills page doesn't use it: there your sign-in only lasts while the page is open, and your browser remembers that you signed in there so Google can offer to sign you back in. Google's privacy policy.

SAVED ONLY IN YOUR BROWSER

Some pages remember things in your browser's own storage on your device. It never leaves your device, and we can't see it: profile reactions, the men you follow (plus a copy of your votes while you're signed in, so they show straight away), which men you've requested, your skills list (also on our server only if you chose Save with Google), your home game plan, your Manly Test results, your Mental Health picks, and whether you've joined the brotherhood and signed up to its code (with the dates). Clear your browser's site data to delete it.

VIDEOS AND LINKS

On the Skills page, the video preview pictures come from YouTube's image server, so Google sees your IP address when you open that page. The videos themselves play in YouTube's privacy-enhanced mode, and only after you press play. Then YouTube (Google) may also set its own cookies. Links to news sites, Wikipedia, Instagram and crisis lines take you to other sites with their own privacy rules.

THE BROTHERHOOD FORUM

The brotherhood forum is a server on Discord, not part of this website. The "Enter the brotherhood forum" button is just a link: nothing is sent to Discord until you click it, and we never see your Discord account or messages through this site. What you post there is handled by Discord, under Discord's privacy policy. As the server's moderators, we can see what you post in the forum, and we may remove posts or members who break its requirements.

DATA OUTSIDE THE EU

GitHub, Google, Cloudflare, Discord and Wikimedia may handle data in the USA. GitHub, Google and Cloudflare take part in the EU–US Data Privacy Framework.

YOUR RIGHTS

You can ask to see, correct or delete your data, to limit how it's used, or object to it being used. Write to humbored.contact@gmail.com. Because we only keep scrambled codes, we usually can't tell which entry is yours, so we'll explain what we can and can't find. You can also complain to a data protection authority, such as the one where you live.

CHANGES

If we change what we keep, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
