# humbored.com

A playful personal site: an 80s disco "ENTER THE MANOSPHERE!!" entry screen leading to "The Real Man Report": a most-wanted-style board of notable men, good and bad, with Facebook-style profiles. The point: ordinary decent men are more masculine than "toxic alpha" influencers.
Hosted on GitHub Pages from `christothejames/christothejames.github.io`; the `CNAME` file points it at humbored.com.

## Tech
- Plain static HTML. Each page has its own `<style>` and `<script>`; the people data lives in `data/people.js`.
- No framework, no build step, no package.json. Pushing to `main` deploys the site.
- Fonts are self-hosted (owner, 27 Oct 2026: no Google Fonts, for GDPR): every page links `fonts/fonts.css` (Bungee, Monoton, Righteous woff2, latin + latin-ext, licence in `fonts/OFL.txt`). Never add a link to fonts.googleapis.com; for a new font, download its woff2 into `fonts/` and add it to `fonts.css`.
- Profile pictures from social media (owner, 31 Oct 2026): a man with no free Commons photo can show his own public profile picture, loaded live (hotlinked, never copied into `images/`): set `facebook` (Wikidata P2013) and/or `tiktok` (P7085) in people.js; the loop at the end of people.js turns that into `photo` (Facebook via graph.facebook.com, TikTok via unavatar.io). Instagram can't be used: there is no free public picture link (unavatar needs a paid key, which would be public here). If the picture fails, home and profile show initials. Listed in privacy.html.
- No other outside scripts on page load. Google's sign-in script (home.html) loads only when the request card appears.

## Run it locally
- Open any `.html` file in a browser, or run `python3 -m http.server 8000` and visit http://localhost:8000

## Shadow ranking (PRIVATE, admins only)
- The shadow points system and ranking page live in a separate **private** repo, `christothejames/humbored-admin` (local folder `../admin`), hosted on Cloudflare Pages at https://humbored-admin.pages.dev behind a Cloudflare Access login (email one-time code). Main app policy "Admins" (Include → Emails) lists who gets in; a second Access app ("humbored-admin - Cloudflare Pages") locks preview URLs `*.humbored-admin.pages.dev`. To add an admin, add their email to the Admins policy. Never put scores, weights, `RULES`, points or men under review in this public repo.
- `admin/shadow.js` – `RULES` (weights), `DIMS`, points for each man, men under review (`list: "review"`), and the scoring. Each fact's points are discounted by age (full if ongoing or from the last 10 years, half if 11–30 years old, a quarter if older, half if undated, so date every fact), then multiplied by fame from `VIEWS` (average monthly English Wikipedia views over the past 12 months: 1,000 or fewer keeps half, 1 million or more keeps all; refresh yearly and add new men to it), then multiplied by influence (owner, 26 Oct 2026: young lads copy the men they see most, so a bigger, hotter platform makes his example count more, good or bad): +0 to +50% for reach from `FOLLOWERS` (biggest social following, Wikidata P8687; 100,000 or fewer adds nothing, 100 million or more adds 50%; missing = no boost; owner, 27 Oct 2026: a man in `OFFLINE`, documented as having no personal social media while still famous, gets his reach from fame instead, 1 million or more monthly views = full +50%, on his good facts only, because staying offline while famous is admirable) and +0 to +50% for trending from `TRENDING` (last 30 days of Wikipedia views against his `VIEWS` average; normal adds nothing, 4× or more adds 50%). Refresh `TRENDING` whenever you rescore; add followers for new men. Not Wanted board order (owner, 26 Oct 2026): ranked by live impact, who is hurting young lads online right now (black marks × current followers and views; men in prison or deceased count for a quarter), so old news like R. Kelly drops below McGregor and Tate; details in admin/CLAUDE.md. Raw points scaled 0–100 across real men; composites aren't scaled; a 0–100 score per dimension, or "no record".
- "How he treats people" dimension (owner, 25 Oct 2026): we want men who give back to ordinary people, not men who take everything and treat people as worthless. Rules: `approachable` (+20, gave his own time or money to an ordinary stranger), `contempt` (−30, publicly demeaned an ordinary person: a worker, a stranger, a disabled person), `tax_dodge` (−150, owner 27 Oct 2026: as heavy as the top kindness, in reverse, because the ranking is about giving back to society; billionaires or top 1% earners only, documented: paid little or no income tax, offshore or tax-exile schemes, or tax fraud; ordinary men's tax trouble stays `unpaid`). When scouting, look for these facts for every man, and for every billionaire or top earner look for tax avoidance (leaked IRS data, Panama/Paradise/Pandora Papers, tax-exile moves, tax cases).
- Underage rule (owner, 26 Oct 2026, revised same day): PROVEN sexual conduct with a minor (a conviction, a civil court finding, or his own admission) puts a man on Not Wanted automatically, whatever his score, with no approval. Allegations, charges and out-of-court settlements are not proof: they are recorded as facts and judged on the score like anything else. Tag every such fact `minor: true` in `admin/shadow.js` (see admin/CLAUDE.md). On the public site, never call an allegation a finding of guilt: say who made it, how old they were, and that he denies it. When scouting, always look for this.
- `admin/index.html` – the ranking page. Loads public facts from https://humbored.com/data/people.js. Each review man shows the board the algorithm suggests (raw ≥ 0 → Wanted, < 0 → Not Wanted) and two approve buttons, plus "approve all as suggested". Each published man has "move to <other board>" and "send back to review". Each pick opens a reason box. Picks and reasons are saved in the admin's browser; "copy for Claude" gives a list (with reasons) to paste back.
- `admin/decisions.md` – every approval/move with the owner's reason. Private; never copy reasons into this repo.
- `admin/publish.mjs` – run `node publish.mjs` in the admin folder after any change to scores or the boards; it rewrites this repo's `data/wires.js`.

## Mental Health wording (important)
- Strategy, not sympathy: "your moves", "quick win", "call in backup". Never imply he's weak, broken or a victim.
- Short sentences a 15-year-old understands first read.
- Getting help is framed as a tactic (pros have coaches), never as failure.
- Anything touching self-harm or suicide must point straight to URGENT HELP (write "tap URGENT HELP" in a move and it gets a red ! and its own button). Urgent situations (`urgent: true`) open the urgent help panel automatically.
- Alcohol: always keep the warning not to stop suddenly if he gets shakes, sweats or sickness without a drink, and to see a doctor.
- Dating advice must be respectful: she's a person, not a target; a no is a no.
- Check any crisis number against an official source before changing it. These are general strategies, not medical advice.
