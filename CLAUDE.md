# humbored.com

A playful personal site: an 80s disco "ENTER THE MANOSPHERE!!" entry screen leading to "The Real Man Report": a most-wanted-style board of notable men, good and bad, with Facebook-style profiles. The point: ordinary decent men are more masculine than "toxic alpha" influencers.
Hosted on GitHub Pages from `christothejames/christothejames.github.io`; the `CNAME` file points it at humbored.com.

## Tech
- Plain static HTML. Each page has its own `<style>` and `<script>`; the people data lives in `data/people.js`.
- No framework, no build step, no package.json. Pushing to `main` deploys the site.
- Only external dependency: Google Fonts.

## Run it locally
- Open any `.html` file in a browser, or run `python3 -m http.server 8000` and visit http://localhost:8000

## Pages
- `index.html` – "ENTER THE MANOSPHERE!!" entry screen: the owner's chosen cartoon-king meme with the king's face swapped for the owner's new cartoon face (`images/entry-meme.jpg`, full with caption) with a pulsing chrome-blue "ENTER THE MANOSPHERE!!" under the staff head, over the synthwave sky, sunset, light reflections and moving blue grid floor. The ENTER button goes to `home.html`. The meme is a TV still the owner chose to use despite copyright risk; don't swap it without asking.
- `home.html` – random hero card (top right of the Wanted board): photo with a slanted red shout banner; each click shows a different hero. Heroes, shouts and photo credits live in `data/heroes.js`, photos in `images/heroes/` (480×480).
- `home.html` – two poster boards: "WANTED: MORE MEN LIKE HIM" (good) and "NOT WANTED" (bad), with search. Each board shows its top 10 in ORDER (best first on Wanted, worst first on Not Wanted); a dropdown shows top 20/30/50 or all, and a search shows every match. Each poster links to a profile.
- `profile.html?id=<id>` – Facebook-style profile: cover, avatar, status line, intro, dated timeline posts with source links, About and Photos tabs, reactions (saved only in the viewer's browser).
  An Instagram button builds a healthy feed: "Follow on Instagram" for Wanted men, "Unfollow on Instagram" (with how-to hint) for Not Wanted men, opening their profile. Not Wanted men banned from Instagram (per their profile text) show "Banned from Instagram". No handle = no button.
- `data/people.js` – public profiles: `list` (good/bad), role, reason, legal/status line, about facts, dated posts with sources, `instagram` handle (from Wikidata P2003, current accounts only).
- `data/wires.js` – GENERATED by the private admin site's `publish.mjs`; never edit by hand. Holds only what public pages need: `DIMS` labels, `WIRES` (per man on the boards, each area +1 good record / −1 black marks) for The Web, and `ORDER` (board order, best first on Wanted, worst first on Not Wanted). No points, weights, scores or men under review.

## Shadow ranking (PRIVATE, admins only)
- The shadow points system and ranking page live in a separate **private** repo, `christothejames/humbored-admin` (local folder `../admin`), hosted on Cloudflare Pages at https://humbored-admin.pages.dev behind a Cloudflare Access login (email one-time code). Main app policy "Admins" (Include → Emails) lists who gets in; a second Access app ("humbored-admin - Cloudflare Pages") locks preview URLs `*.humbored-admin.pages.dev`. To add an admin, add their email to the Admins policy. Never put scores, weights, `RULES`, points or men under review in this public repo.
- `admin/shadow.js` – `RULES` (weights), `DIMS`, points for each man, men under review (`list: "review"`), and the scoring. Each fact's points are discounted by age (full if ongoing or from the last 10 years, half if 11–30 years old, a quarter if older, half if undated, so date every fact), then multiplied by fame from `VIEWS` (average monthly English Wikipedia views over the past 12 months: 1,000 or fewer keeps half, 1 million or more keeps all; refresh yearly and add new men to it). Raw points scaled 0–100 across real men; composites aren't scaled; a 0–100 score per dimension, or "no record".
- "How he treats people" dimension (owner, 25 Oct 2026): we want men who give back to ordinary people, not men who take everything and treat people as worthless. Rules: `approachable` (+20, gave his own time or money to an ordinary stranger), `contempt` (−30, publicly demeaned an ordinary person: a worker, a stranger, a disabled person), `tax_dodge` (−20, paid no income tax in a year while a billionaire). When scouting, look for these facts for every man.
- `admin/index.html` – the ranking page. Loads public facts from https://humbored.com/data/people.js. Each review man shows the board the algorithm suggests (raw ≥ 0 → Wanted, < 0 → Not Wanted) and two approve buttons, plus "approve all as suggested". Each published man has "move to <other board>" and "send back to review". Each pick opens a reason box. Picks and reasons are saved in the admin's browser; "copy for Claude" gives a list (with reasons) to paste back.
- `admin/decisions.md` – every approval/move with the owner's reason. Private; never copy reasons into this repo.
- `admin/publish.mjs` – run `node publish.mjs` in the admin folder after any change to scores or the boards; it rewrites this repo's `data/wires.js`.

## Adding people in bulk (e.g. "pull 50 notable men from Wikipedia")
0. Read `admin/decisions.md` first. Use the owner's past reasons to judge who is worth scouting and which documented facts to look for. Reasons never become points: points stay sourced facts. If reasons keep overriding the algorithm the same way, propose a `RULES` weight change to the owner; don't change weights without asking.
1. Add each to `SHADOW_PEOPLE` in `admin/shadow.js` as `list: "review"`, with points that are documented facts only, each with a source link. No opinions, no adjectives about character. Push the admin repo.
2. The algorithm scores and suggests a board automatically; the owner approves on the admin site.
3. When the owner pastes "Publish these shadow approvals: …": recheck anything legal (charges, verdicts, appeals) is current; set each man's `list` in `admin/shadow.js`; add his public profile to `data/people.js` (posts = his sourced facts, plain wording, no scores or rule names; a Commons photo and credit where one exists); append each man and his `reason:` line to `admin/decisions.md` (format at the top of that file); run `node publish.mjs`; push both repos.
   - Lines marked "(move from Wanted/Not Wanted)" are published men: "→ Wanted"/"→ Not Wanted" switches his `list` in both `admin/shadow.js` and `data/people.js` (reword his profile for the other board if needed); "→ back to review" sets `list: "review"` in shadow.js and removes his profile (and photo) from the public repo.
- `plan.html` – "Mental Health" (formerly The Game Plan): pick an area (pressure, low mood, anger, alcohol, dating, relationship, friendships, purpose, sleep, modern day problems: doom scrolling, bot farms/fake accounts, touch grass), then a situation, and get a strategy: moves, a quick win today, when to call in backup and who. Red BACKUP NOW button opens crisis lines by country. Deep links: `plan.html#alcohol/cut-back`.
- `web.html` – "The Web": interactive force graph of every man on the boards, wired to the six `DIMS` hubs (gold wire = good record there, red = black marks). No numbers shown; wires come from `data/wires.js`. Linked from the home toolbar.
- `data/plans.js` – all Mental Health strategies (`AREAS`) and crisis lines (`BACKUP`).
- `skills.html` – "The Skills": a "Wherever you started" paragraph, then everyday life skills with lazy-loaded YouTube tutorials, category filters, and "★ LEARN" (saves the skill and starts its video) / "✓ ALREADY KNOW" buttons on each card. Already know asks "Sure you already know this one?" first; YES hides the skill from the main "To learn" tab, and it waits in the "Already know" tab with "↺ PUT BACK". Tabs: To learn / Learn / Already know / Not sorted yet (saved only in the viewer's browser). Linked from the home toolbar by the arcade-style "▶ LEARN A SKILL +XP" button. Skills live in `data/skills.js` (optional `start`/`end` seconds clip a video).
- `images/` – profile photos, 600×600 JPG around 50 KB. Set `photo`, `photoCredit` (author, licence, "via Wikimedia Commons") and `photoSource` (the Commons file page) in people.js; credits show on the profile and in the home footer. Never use mugshots or arrest photos of anyone not convicted.
- `CNAME` – custom domain. Never delete or change it.

## Style
- All pages share one blue 80s disco look (bold, macho, welcoming); keep new pages in the same style.
  - Colors: navy night sky `#050b1a`, electric blue `#1e90ff`, sky blue `#7cc4ff`, neon cyan `#3ef2ff`; gold `#ffe66d` for the good board, red `#ff3b3b` for the bad board. Fonts: Bungee (bold headings), Monoton (neon titles), Righteous (body).
- Tone: fun, silly, energetic. Short punchy text.
- Must work on phones (touch, small screens). Respect `prefers-reduced-motion` for heavy animation.

## Mental Health wording (important)
- Strategy, not sympathy: "your moves", "quick win", "call in backup". Never imply he's weak, broken or a victim.
- Short sentences a 15-year-old understands first read.
- Getting help is framed as a tactic (pros have coaches), never as failure.
- Anything touching self-harm or suicide must point straight to BACKUP NOW. Urgent situations (`urgent: true`) open the backup panel automatically.
- Alcohol: always keep the warning not to stop suddenly if he gets shakes, sweats or sickness without a drink, and to see a doctor.
- Dating advice must be respectful: she's a person, not a target; a no is a no.
- Check any crisis number against an official source before changing it. These are general strategies, not medical advice.

## Rules for Claude
- Keep pages self-contained single HTML files unless asked otherwise. No build tools or frameworks without asking.
- New pages: link to them from `home.html` so they can be found.
- Keep images compressed; keep the site fast.
- Only public figures or clearly labelled composites on the site, never private individuals.
- Every factual claim about a real person needs a source link. Charges are allegations: say "charged with" and note if he denies them. Check legal status is current before editing a profile.
- Shadow scores are private to admins. They exist only in the private admin repo behind the login; the public site has no shadow mode.
- Scoring uses the Ten Commandments about how people are treated (murder, theft/fraud, adultery, lying, greed, parents), never religious belief or practice. Only count infidelity he admitted or a court established; unproven allegations are worth little and must say he denies them.
- New men go in as `review` until the owner decides good or bad.
- Score a politician only on his personal record, never his politics or policies.
- Posters for people charged but not convicted must show their current legal status and that the board is opinion, not a finding of guilt.
- Only use photos we have the right to use (e.g. Wikimedia Commons) and fill in `photoCredit`.
- After changes, open the page in a browser and check it on a narrow (phone) width before saying it's done.
