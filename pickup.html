The moves, unmasked

Every one of these is sold as confidence. Look closer and it's a way to get round what she actually wants.

  • Negging

    Sold as: a backhanded compliment that knocks her down a peg, so she works for your approval.

    Really: putting someone down on purpose so they feel worse about themselves. That's how bullies work.

  • Push-pull

    Sold as: run hot, then cold, so she never knows where she stands.

    Really: making someone anxious on purpose. Keeping a person off balance is control, not chemistry.

  • Isolating the target

    Sold as: get her away from her friends, they'll only "block" you.

    Really: cutting her off from the people looking out for her. Her friends aren't obstacles. Notice the word "target".

  • Kino escalation

    Sold as: touch her a bit more each time and see what she lets you get away with.

    Really: testing how far you can push before she stops you. Touch needs a yes, not a lack of a no.

  • False time limit

    Sold as: say "I can only stay a minute" so she drops her guard.

    Really: lying to get past her guard. If the opener is a lie, what's the rest?

  • "Last minute resistance"

    Sold as: her "no" is just a hurdle. Keep going and get past it.

    Really: a no is a no. Pushing past it is pressure, and it can be a crime. Anyone teaching this is teaching you to ignore consent.

  • Peacocking

    Sold as: wear something loud so women can't ignore you.

    Really: the only harmless one on the list. Wear what you like. Just wear it because you like it.
